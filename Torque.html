Overview

The intention of this project is to use concepts I am currently learning in Dynamics and Ordinary differential equations to expand on my previous robotic arm project. In said previous project I limited myself to servo actuators however, a more practical actuator would be a motor with a cleverly designed gearbox. Learning how to properly control torque in nonlinear systems is the first step in implementing this new actuator approach.
